About NEO WEST

A specialized scientific meeting dedicated to neonatal respiratory care, neuroprotection and better outcomes for our smallest patients.

Our Vision

To create a high-impact regional platform where neonatal and respiratory care teams exchange practical knowledge, strengthen collaboration and accelerate innovation.

Our Mission

Bring together clinicians, educators and innovators through expert sessions, workshops and simulation-based learning that can translate directly into safer bedside care.

Who Should Attend?

  • Neonatologists and pediatricians
  • NICU nurses and respiratory therapists
  • Healthcare professionals involved in neonatal care
  • Researchers, educators and clinical innovators

Conference Theme

Little Lungs, Growing Minds
Advancing Neonatal Respiratory Care & Neuroprotection.
